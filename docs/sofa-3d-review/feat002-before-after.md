# FEAT-002 — Assembled piece + five cushions: soft contact, cradle, neighbour separation

Parametric edits to `assets/sofa-pilot/source/build_sofa.py`, re-exported to both GLBs and the
regenerated `source/sofa.blend` in one generator pass (bpy 5.0.1). No baked GLB surgery, no
exploded-view offset compensation. Revision bumped `1.0.0 -> 1.1.0`.

## What changed (and why), per the surface-based inspection conclusion (I.4)

The inspection corrected the earlier AABB reading: cushions are NOT buried 9-12 cm; on the real
meshes each only grazes the seat shell along a thin contact band (254-448 overlapping triangle
pairs, ~0% embedded volume), while NEIGHBOUR cushions genuinely interpenetrate (122-158 tri-pairs).
So FEAT-002 added the soft contact that did not exist in the source and relieved neighbour overlap,
without large vertical moves that would harm the silhouette.

1. **Contact-flatten in `pillow()` (new topology).** Added a `flatten` parameter. On the underside
   face (local `side=+1`, which the side-probe confirmed is the lower face for all five tilted
   cushions), a broad central contact patch compresses the belly toward flat, as foam does under
   load, while the tapered knife-edge perimeter and the welt seam are preserved. Per-cushion
   `flatten` 0.66-0.70.
2. **Seat cradle in `seat()` and the `seat_panel_seam` tube.** Replaced the single 9 mm Gaussian
   (fixed at x=-0.48) with a sum of shallow 19 mm dimples, one per cushion footprint, clamped to
   26 mm, driven by the shared module-level `CUSHIONS` list so the dimples track the real cushion
   placements. The seat crown now cradles each cushion so the thin contact band reads as a seam,
   not a shell intersection.
3. **Neighbour separation (small pos/size nudges only).** Cushion X positions nudged outward a few
   mm at the ends and half-widths trimmed ~2 mm; no large `pos.z` moves. Composition (three light +
   two olive-weave, asymmetry, chaise on the right) preserved. Per-cushion COLOUR remains FEAT-005.

The `CUSHIONS` literals replaced the inline `pillows` list in `build()`; `pillow()` is called with
`flatten=flat`. Material names/bindings (`fabric_main`, `fabric_seam` via `cloth.copy()`) unchanged.

The contact band is softened (not forced to zero — a thin band is natural and now reads as
compression), no new deep penetration was introduced, and every neighbour overlap decreased.
Silhouette and footprint are essentially unchanged.
